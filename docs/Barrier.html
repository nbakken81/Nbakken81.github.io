Method. Primary: B = P d²/(W U T), d from the target. Leakage: B = P dL²/(10⁻³ WL T), WL = W[(1 − f) + f CI], d from isocenter. Patient scatter: B = P dsca² dsec² 400/(a W T F). n = −log10B; t = TVL1 + (n − 1)TVLe. Secondary barriers combine the two components: if their thicknesses differ by at least one TVL, the larger is used; otherwise one HVL is added to the larger.

Data. Built-in TVLs and scatter fractions are from general knowledge of NCRP 151 Tables B.2, B.4, B.5a and B.7 and must be confirmed against the report. Steel and lead leakage TVLs are set equal to their primary values, and density-scaled materials scale ordinary-concrete TVLs by 2.35/ρ; both are conservative approximations, especially for high-Z materials at scatter energies.

Not included. Photoneutrons above 10 MV, maze and door design, laminated barriers, and skyshine. This is a checking and teaching aid, not a substitute for a shielding report by a qualified expert.
